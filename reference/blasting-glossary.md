# Blasting Glossary — Kirra in Every Language

Kirra's interface is available in eight language editions. Blasting terms are **mining terms of art**, not dictionary words: *Burden* is the row-to-row distance to the free face, not a load; *Grade* is the floor elevation, not a slope; *Toe* and *Collar* are the ends of the hole.

This page lists the term Kirra uses for each concept in each edition. Use it to check a label, to learn the equivalent term in another language, or to tell us where a term doesn't sound right on your site.

---

## Choosing a language

Pick a language from **Select Language** in the top bar, or from the **Language** group in the side panel. Kirra switches straight away and remembers your choice.

| Menu entry | Edition |
|---|---|
| English - English | English |
| Chinese - 中文 | Simplified Chinese (mainland China) |
| French (France) - Français (France) | French as used in France |
| French (Québec) - Français (Québec) | Québec French — regional mining terms |
| Mongolian - Монгол | Mongolian (Cyrillic) |
| Russian - Русский | Russian |
| Spanish (Spain) - Español (España) | Spanish as used in Spain |
| Spanish (Latin America) - Español (Latinoamérica) | Latin American Spanish — regional mining terms |

The regional editions only change the words that differ in the region — for example **fardeau** and **sautage** in Québec, **taladro** and **factor de carga** in Latin America. Everything else is shared with French (France) or Spanish (Spain).

Exported files keep their standard column names in every language, so a file saved in one language opens anywhere.

| French (Québec) | Québec DEP 5369 *Forage et dynamitage* curriculum |
| Spanish (Spain) | IGME and López Jimeno blasting references |
| Spanish (Latin America) | SONAMI / SERNAGEOMIN (Chile) and Peru DS 024-2016-EM |
| Russian | ГОСТ Р 57704-2017 and Russian bench-blasting guides and safety rules |
| Mongolian | Mongolia's 2018 blasting safety rules and MUST mining proceedings |

`—` in a regional column below means the term is the same as the base language.

Corrections from practising blasters are the most valuable feedback the translations can get.
